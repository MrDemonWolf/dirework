DireWork is a self-hosted Pomodoro timer and task list for Twitch co-working and body-doubling streams. Streamers sign in with Twitch, connect a bot account, configure OBS overlays, and their viewers interact via chat commands.

Each instance has one streamer account and one channel. Viewers use chat commands;
they do not need dashboard accounts. Keep one bot page open (an invisible widget in OBS
or Moblin, or the dock in OBS or a browser tab) while using chat commands.

The core app supports Cloudflare's free tier, within account quotas. Free-tier
support is a project commitment; no paid Cloudflare service is required.

**Start here:** [deploy your instance](/docs/deployment), claim it with Twitch,
connect your bot, then add the OBS sources. To try it on your computer first,
use [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).

DireWork is always self-hosted: you deploy and operate your own instance. MrDemonWolf, Inc. does not offer hosted instances or a managed hosting service.
