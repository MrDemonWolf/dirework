Self-hosted UI font files for the Dirework dashboard (apps/web) and docs site
(apps/fumadocs), loaded at build time through next/font/local. All files in
files/ are latin subsets of Google Fonts families, downloaded by
apps/web/scripts/fetch-fonts.ts and redistributed under their original
license. The full license text ships alongside this file:
